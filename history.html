From Missouri to the heavy-music scene

Poetry On Fire is a Christian alternative rock and metalcore band from Cape Girardeau, Missouri, fronted by vocalist, songwriter, and producer Kaleb Webb.

Webb's musical journey includes nearly two decades of writing and production experience, from early home recordings to performing in the Christian rock and metalcore scene. Poetry On Fire channels that background into songs about faith, struggle, purpose, and restoration.

The group's sound mixes technical guitar work, driving percussion, atmospheric passages, melodic hooks, and heavy breakdowns. Their message centers on hope through Jesus Christ.
